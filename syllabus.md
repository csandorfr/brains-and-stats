# Syllabus

Two sessions of 45 minutes: 30 minutes of theory, 15 minutes in R.

## Session 1

1. **Descriptive statistics and visualisation**
   Mean, median, mode; range, variance, SD; histograms, bar charts,
   boxplots; outliers and robustness; what SD, SEM and 95% confidence
   interval error bars each mean.
2. **Probability and expected value**
   The 0 to 1 scale; addition and multiplication rules; independence;
   expected value and the law of large numbers.
3. **Distributions and the central limit theorem**
   Binomial, Poisson, normal; sampling distributions; why averaging
   reduces noise.

Scripts `01` to `03`. Quick-check questions in `exercises/`.

## Session 2

4. **Hypothesis testing and p-values**
   Null and alternative hypotheses; the courtroom analogy; empirical
   p-values by simulation; one- and two-sided tests.
5. **Errors, power and sample size**
   Type I and Type II errors; power curves; planning n; precision vs power;
   pseudoreplication and what your n actually is.
6. **Parametric vs non-parametric**
   Assumptions; t-tests vs Wilcoxon and Mann-Whitney; what each actually
   tests.
7. **Multiple testing and effect size**
   Familywise error; Bonferroni and FDR; Cohen's d; correlation r.

Scripts `04` to `07`. Quick-check questions in `exercises/`.

## The running example

Tasty Beer is accused of underfilling its 0.5 L bottles. The question is
posed in session 1 and answered in session 2, where ten bottles turn out
to be too few to tell, whatever the machine is doing. Script `04` works it
through.
