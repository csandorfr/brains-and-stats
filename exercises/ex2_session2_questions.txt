Q1  Hypotheses and p-values
    You compare hippocampal volume in patients and controls.
    - State the null hypothesis.
    - Put p = 0.01 into words, WITHOUT using the phrase "probability the
      hypothesis is true".

Q2  Multiple testing
    You run 20 independent tests, each with a false positive rate of 0.05.
    - Compute the probability of at least one false positive.
    - What threshold would a Bonferroni correction set?
    - Why might an effect size be more useful than a p-value here?

Q3a Non-parametric data: number of siblings
    In a class of 30 students, the number of siblings follows a Poisson
    distribution with mean 1.6.
    - Simulate 30 values and plot a histogram.
    - Calculate the median and interquartile range.
    - Why might a non-parametric test be more appropriate here?

Q3b Paired data: pre- and post-intervention
    Reaction times (ms) are measured before and after an intervention in
    20 participants.
    - Simulate pre values from a normal distribution (mean 320, SD 40).
    - Generate post values on average 25 ms faster, with variability.
    - Test whether the difference is significant using a paired t-test.
    - Why is a paired test the right choice here?

Q4  Underpowered studies
    Why is an underpowered study problematic in neuroscience?
    Discussion question. Think about what happens to the results it
    produces, in both directions.
