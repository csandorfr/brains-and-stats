(numbering matches the slide)

Q1  Why do we need statistics in neuroscience?
    Discussion question. Think about three separate sources of variation:
    biological differences between animals, measurement noise, and small
    samples.

Q2  Reaction times
    A neuroscientist records five reaction times (ms): 220, 230, 400, 240, 250
    - Calculate the mean and the median.
    - Which is more robust to the outlier?
    - Which would you report, and why?

Q3  Expected number of spikes
    A neuron fires with probability 0.2 on each of 10 independent trials.
    - Compute the expected number of spikes.

Q4  Coin flipping simulation
    You flip a fair coin 5 times and record the proportion of heads, then
    repeat the experiment 1000 times.
    - Simulate the proportion of heads in each experiment.
    - Plot the histogram of these proportions.
    - What changes if you flip 50 times instead of 5?
